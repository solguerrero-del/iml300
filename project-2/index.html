Growing Up With Depression

I find comfort in my depression.

I do not remember a life without depression.

The darkness of my depression keeps me warm,
it swaddles me till I can't breathe.

She is my safety blanket,
and I hug right back.


This is who I am, these are the feeling I was born with.






Do you ever feel like this?

You were born with these feelings?

What does that feel like?
