My memory has been fogged by my depression. For as long as I can remember I knew there was always something out of place for me. I didn't feel safe within my skin.

My depression was so presistent the feeling became second nature. Rather than allowing myself to heal, I came to the thought that this feeling would never end. And in all reality, I was somewhat true.

My depression blocks me from accepting the warm, it does more harm than good. But I know what harm feels like, and I'm far more used to it than good. But still I stand, despite feeling forever stuck.
